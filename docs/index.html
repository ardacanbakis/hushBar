// How it works

Dead simple by design

No menus to dig through, no system preferences — just a badge in your menu bar (or Windows system tray) and one click.

01
Click the badge

Left-click the hushBar badge in your menu bar or system tray — or hit ⇧⌘M (⌃⇧M on Windows) from any app. No special permissions needed.

02
Every app goes silent

hushBar flips a hardware mute flag. Zoom, Discord, browsers, DAWs — every app is silenced at once.

03
The sign confirms it

Your menu bar badge switches to its muted label — "HUSH", "Shhh", whatever you chose — so you always know your status at a glance.

// Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't

01
Truly global mute

Toggles a hardware device flag — every app that uses your mic is silenced simultaneously, not one by one.

02
Custom bar styles

Pill, rounded rect, toggle switch, mic icon, or ON AIR plaque. Set colors, text and capitalization per preset.

03
Global shortcut

Default ⇧⌘M (⌃⇧M on Windows) works from any app — fully remappable in Preferences. No extra permissions required.

04
Never records

hushBar only flips a hardware flag. No audio stream is ever opened, captured, or transmitted — ever.

05
Launch at login

Enable once and forget. Starts silently in the background — no Dock icon, no splash screen, zero friction.

06
Sound feedback

Optional audio cue on toggle — Pop, Tink, Glass, Hero and more — or keep it completely silent.
