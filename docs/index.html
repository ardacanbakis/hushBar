How it works

Dead simple by design

No menus to dig through. No system preferences. Just a badge in your menu bar and one click.

1
Click the badge

Left-click the hushBar badge in your menu bar — or use your keyboard shortcut (default ⇧⌘M) from any app.

2
Every app goes silent

hushBar flips a hardware mute flag on your Mac. Zoom, Discord, browsers, DAWs — every app is silenced at once.

3
Badge confirms it

The menu bar badge switches to your muted label — "Hush", "Shhh", whatever you chose — so you always know your status.

Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't

hushBar is deliberately small. It does one thing extremely well.

🌐
Truly global mute

Toggles a hardware device flag — every app that uses your microphone is silenced simultaneously, not one by one.

🎨
Custom bar styles

Pick from pill, rounded rect, rectangle, toggle switch, or mic icon. Set colors, text, and capitalization per preset.

⌨️
Global shortcut

Default ⇧⌘M works from any app — no Accessibility permission needed. Fully remappable in Preferences.

🔒
Never records

hushBar only flips a hardware flag. No audio stream is ever opened, captured, or transmitted — ever.

🚀
Launch at login

Enable once and forget. Starts silently in the background — no Dock icon, no splash screen, zero friction.

🔔
Sound feedback

Optional audio cue on toggle. Choose from Pop, Tink, Glass, Hero, and more — or keep it completely silent.
